## 4. The customer
- Types (Roro): homeowners, landlords and investors, syndics (VME), businesses. The page speaks to the homeowner in an emergency first, because that is the search that converts on a phone; syndics and landlords get one short block.
- Age, two views and one read (2026-09-28). Roro by phone: first 40 to 60, then one caller of about 25 to 30 and one of about 70. Fady's own impression: "mostly old", 65 and more. Google Ads, all time, 183 clicks (research/57): the age is known for 124 of them; 65+ is the largest group (43 clicks, 35 percent of the known), 55 or older make 60 percent, 45 to 64 make 40 percent; women 72 clicks and 7 taps on Call, men 51 and 1. These are the people who CLICK the ad; who calls and who becomes a job is not measured, and 9 taps are too few to rank groups. No age, gender or device is excluded or adjusted in the account: Roro's thought that an age setting had been chosen is answered (Fady's word: he confused it with the first sitting, where the landlord as a likely caller was discussed).
- Language: mixed FR and NL. Only 2 of 16 competitors are bilingual (`research/02` 3.3). Both languages, first class.
- Zone (Roro via Fady, corrected 2026-08-23; widened 2026-09-28 to add Brussels): all 19 communes of the Brussels-Capital Region are now in the zone too, same prices, travel included, nothing added (Roro's yes by phone, Fady's picks by widget, DECISIONS 2026-09-28); the van is Euro 6, so it may drive in the low emission zone. Plus the ring, about 40 km around WEMMEL on both sides of the language border, Wallonia included (Wemmel, Meise, Vilvoorde, Zaventem, Dilbeek, Halle, Waterloo, Nivelles, Wavre, ...). Town list proposal: `launch-plan.md` section 6.
- Pain points, in order (`research/02` 4.4 and 4.5): (1) the invoice that is not the phone price, (2) nobody comes or comes hours late (sometimes on purpose to hit the night rate), (3) damage to pipes and tiles, (4) the night price invented on the spot, (5) cowboys: a documented scam network with 265+ victims 2020 to 2025 in the Halle-Vilvoorde district, our exact home market, (6) the job not really fixed.
- What consumers are told to check, so the page shows it unasked: the enterprise number, the VAT number, a written quote, the hourly or forfait price, a named human and a branded van.
- The journey: phone in hand, short urgent search, they call two or three numbers, whoever answers with a time and a price wins. An unanswered phone is a lost job; a slow page or a cookie wall is a lost job.
